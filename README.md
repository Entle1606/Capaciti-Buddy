# CAPACITI Candidate Support Chatbot 🤖

A conversational chatbot designed to help CAPACITI candidates navigate their programme experience with quick, accessible information in one place.

The chatbot acts as a virtual guide for candidates, providing information about **CAPACITI life, programme guidelines, house rules, support contacts, and general candidate resources**. It is intended to reduce the time candidates spend searching for information and make it easier to know where to go or who to approach when they need assistance.

### Key Areas

*  **Programme Overview** – Understand the CAPACITI programme, structure, expectations, and key information.
*  **Life at CAPACITI** – Guidance on day-to-day programme life and what candidates can expect.
*  **House Rules** – Easy access to important rules, policies, and candidate responsibilities.
*  **Who to Talk To** – Helps candidates identify the appropriate person or team to contact for different queries or concerns.
*  **General FAQs** – Answers common questions candidates may have throughout the programme.
*  **Candidate Navigation** – Provides a central starting point for finding relevant information and resources.

### Goal

The goal of the chatbot is to make the CAPACITI experience **easier to navigate, more accessible, and less overwhelming**, especially for new candidates who may not know where to find information or who to approach.

> **One chatbot. One place. Less confusion.**
